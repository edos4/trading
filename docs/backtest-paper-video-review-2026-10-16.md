# Backtest and paper-stream review — 2026-10-16

Verdict: **Do not treat the current backtest or historical paper results as evidence of a deployable trading edge yet.** Historical-stream is the better foundation, but execution and validation gaps remain. This review changes no application code and evaluates the current working tree, including existing uncommitted work.

## Video studied

[How To Backtest Properly With Claude Code (MUST WATCH), AI Pathways](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lIMu8ysJW68), 16m30s. Downloaded video, English automatic captions, and metadata to `/tmp/trading-video-review/`. Read the full captions and inspected sampled video frames.

The useful checklist is: choose parameters using training data only; freeze them before testing later unseen periods; combine only test-period returns; calculate indicators using information available at the time; and include fees/slippage. The demonstrated 12-month/3-month split is an example, not a required setting. Neither complex indicators nor a walk-forward result guarantees future returns. Bar-by-bar replay and rolling train/test evaluation are different things. A fixed strategy can be evaluated on a genuinely untouched holdout without adding an optimizer.

## Must fix before interpreting results as realistic execution

1. **Legacy offline detection uses future information.** `core/backtester.py:936` seeds the complete history and activates historical mode. In `patterns/002_double_top.py:218`, historical evaluation searches up to 30 bars after the second peak for a neckline break, but can backdate entry to day seven. A break on day 20 can therefore authorize a day-seven trade. The causal paper path explicitly behaves differently. Existing `patterns/test_live_detection.py::test_live_double_top_day_seven_does_not_require_future_break` demonstrates this distinction and passed. **Smallest direction:** use historical-stream for strategy evaluation; keep legacy numbers explicitly labeled as historical replication, or migrate the offline path to the causal runner. Otherwise selection bias survives even when exit helpers are identical.

2. **Offline histories above 512 bars are misindexed.** `core/backtester.py:912` creates a 512-bar store, while `data/ohlcv_store.py:84` retains only its final 512 bars. The outer walk still indexes the original full tape. An assert-based 600-bar probe confirmed walk bar 30 had close 130, but the detector's bar 30 had close 218; walk index 512 is outside the detector frame. **Smallest fix:** retain the full tape for the legacy adapter and test more than 512 bars. This fixes alignment only; it does not fix finding 1. Otherwise multi-year results can use the wrong dates/prices or fail in a detector.

3. **Legacy exits can use impossible fills and same-bar hindsight.** `core/backtester.py:654` fills a gap-through stop at its trigger, regardless of the opening price. Reproduced: long entry 100, stop 95, next OHLC 90/92/85/88 exits at 95, above that day's entire range. At `core/backtester.py:651`, eligible legacy trails update from today's close before checking today's earlier high/low. Reproduced: OHLC 100/111/99/110 creates a 104.5 trailing stop from close 110 and then executes against low 99. **Smallest direction:** use gap-aware fills and establish close-based trails for the next bar, with pinned-version migration handled explicitly. Patternsv2 already has these protections; do not regress that branch. Legacy backtests and legacy paper positions both inherit this issue.

4. **Closed-bar signals are filled at that same bar's close.** `core/scanner.py:911` waits for a closed session bar; `core/scanner.py:1440` subsequently fills at that bar's close. `core/stream_backtest.py:346` makes the same assumption. For a signal requiring the final close, a close of 100 followed by an open of 110 is modeled as entry at 100. Adding fixed slippage does not model that overnight gap. **Smallest direction:** queue close-confirmed signals for the next executable bar/open, or explicitly label current results as idealized signal-close simulations. Otherwise matching backtest/paper results still do not establish executable performance.

5. **Open positions omit already-incurred entry fees from equity/net P&L.** `core/paper_trader.py:707` moves only notional at entry; both commissions are charged at exit. `core/stream_backtest.py:473` likewise omits open-position entry costs, although its artifacts count those costs in `fees`. Reproduced with a $10,000 flat-price entry and 0.1% fee: reported fees $10, equity $100,000, unrealized/net P&L $0. **Smallest fix:** charge entry fees when opened and only the remaining exit fee when closed; reconcile stream marks, exports, and closed-trade accounting to avoid double charging. Otherwise keep-open results and drawdowns overstate account value.

## Validation gaps and intentional limits

6. **The legacy parity test does not exercise causal paper detection.** `tests/test_backtest_paper_parity.py:67` supplies the full history and line 72 activates historical-walk mode on its paper side. Passing this test proves matching fills under that artificial setup, not matching signals from actual streaming. **Fix:** retain the helper-level test but add scanner/stream equivalence with progressively supplied history, matching gates, ranking, costs, and versions. Otherwise the test conceals finding 1.

7. **The provided launcher and service backtest do not run the same selection policy.** `paper_stream.sh:18` uses `--volume-gate --collect-first=4`; `core/backtest_service.py:182` rejects collect-first altogether, and offline mode also rejects the volume gate. This is an explicit feature limit, not silent failure. **Action:** compare only matching supported configurations, or implement the same per-session top-four selection in replay before claiming launcher parity. Do not run the launcher casually: it also includes `--paper-reset`.

8. **No rolling train/freeze/test evaluation was found in the reviewed backtest paths.** `StreamReplay` runs a fixed version set over one selected interval; this is causal replay, not the video's rolling optimization experiment. **Action:** freeze rules before an untouched chronological evaluation period; if tuning parameters, evaluate successive training/test folds and report only test results. Keep a final holdout untouched by strategy revisions. Otherwise repeatedly choosing patterns or settings on the reported period can overfit even a causal engine.

9. **Pattern books intentionally allow unconstrained flat-notional positions.** `core/paper_trader.py:644` documents removal of cash, exposure, and portfolio constraints; stream replay likewise tracks independent trades. This can be appropriate for signal research, but is not evidence that a finite account could take all trades. **Action:** label independent-trade results clearly; model account limits before using account returns to judge deployability.

## What is sound in the inspected design

Historical-stream grows its candle store progressively, freezes its dataset/version inputs through the service, and separates warmup from trading. Paper filters exit checks by timeframe, persists processed bar identities, and has recovery/lifecycle tests. Patternsv2 tests cover gap fills, target touches, ambiguous-bar stop priority, trailing activation using prior closes, persistence, and cash/P&L reconciliation after closing. Fees and slippage exist; their presence alone does not resolve the execution issues above.
