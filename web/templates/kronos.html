Experiment 2 — 5-day close accuracy

Fully automated — no button click is required. A weekday cron entry (scripts/kronos_experiment2_daily.sh) runs at 17:00 America/New_York: it grades settled rows first, then forecasts the tracked symbols for 5 trading days and stores the +1D and +5D rows. After the one-week save window it keeps grading daily for ten more days so the final D5 rows settle, then removes itself. Accuracy is 100 − |predicted − actual| / actual × 100. Settled rows are listed first; a row stays pending until its predicted date's US close prints. Use Graded only to hide pending rows.
